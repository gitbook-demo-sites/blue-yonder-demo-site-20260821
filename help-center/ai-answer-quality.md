# AI answer quality

Guidelines for making supply-chain content easier for AI agents to answer from.

{% hint style="info" icon="gitbook-assistant" %}
Use the Assistant on this page to ask how this topic affects planners, operators, developers, and partners.

## What to document

* Lead with direct summaries.
* Name roles, systems, and operational states consistently.
* Keep examples close to the page they support.
* Avoid burying important constraints in PDFs or gated files only.
